Historical covering witnesses and catalogue metadata
===================================================

witnesses/65-13-2.json preserves the 31-block La Jolla Covering Repository
construction credited to Flavio Perona, 21 May 2011, 03:32:48 (method blank).
witnesses/95-19-2.json preserves the 31-block construction credited to
Jan de Heer and Steve Muir, "Private tools", 16 March 2011, 12:50:04.
LJCR is curated by Daniel M. Gordon.

The historical witnesses were recovered from the read-only CVKT archive.
The adaptation changes compressed uint8 rows to JSON and adds provenance
fields; original labels and block order are unchanged. These are not new
constructions by this project. Original compressed bytes, metadata and
source observations are retained under inputs/. The full LJCR metadata
snapshot is retained unchanged as inputs/coverdata.json.
