Historical covering witness
===========================

witnesses/44-11-2.json preserves the twenty-one-block La Jolla Covering
Repository construction credited to Colin Barker, "Simulated Annealing",
1 December 2008, 09:10:02. LJCR is curated by Daniel M. Gordon.
It was recovered from the read-only CVKT archive. The adaptation changes
compressed uint8 rows to JSON and adds provenance fields; original labels
and block order are unchanged. It is not a new construction by this project.
The archived source bytes and metadata are retained under inputs/.
